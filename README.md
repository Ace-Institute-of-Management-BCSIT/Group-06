# Group-06
Initial Project Ideas by Group 6:
1. **HealthBridge** — Local Doctor Appointment & Record System
A platform to book doctor appointments, track personal health history and receive medication or checkup reminders — all in one place.

2. **MentalSpace** — Mental Wellness & Mood Tracking App
Track your daily mood, write journal entries, and connect with licensed counselors — a safe space to prioritize your mental health.

3. **ServiceHub** — Local Service Finder & Booking App
Find and book trusted local services like tutors, plumbers, and electricians. Compare reviews, check availability, and hire — all in one place.

4. **CampusConnect** — Student Community & Resource Hub
A student-focused platform to find lecture notes, discover campus events, join clubs, and access academic and social services — all in one place.

5. **VolunteerHub** — Volunteering & Social Impact Platform
Connects individuals with nearby volunteering opportunities based on their interests, skills, and availability. Browse verified NGOs, sign up for events, track hours, earn digital certificates, and view your impact through a personal dashboard.

6. **StockSmart** — Supermarket & Warehouse Inventory Management System
A management tool for supermarkets and warehouses to track stock levels, monitor expiry dates, manage suppliers, and generate restocking alerts — keeping operations efficient and loss-free.
